Why you can rely on it

Three rules Arbora never breaks

AI that helps you study is only worth using if you can trust what it makes. These rules are enforced in the code, not just promised.

Grounded & cited

Every note, card, and quiz item is generated only from your retrieved sources and carries a citation to the exact page or timestamp. No source, no item.

You review before you trust

AI output is staged, never silently added to your deck. You approve each item in a review gate, so nothing enters your study set without your say-so.

Local-first, private

The core never sends your data off your device. It runs a local model through Ollama; using an online key is an explicit, optional opt-in.

How it works

From your files to lasting memory

Four calm steps. No pressure, no streak anxiety, and a tree that only ever grows.

1

Add your material

Drop in PDFs, slides, documents, and lecture recordings or videos. Arbora transcribes and indexes them, subject by subject.

2

Generate grounded study items

Ask for notes, flashcards, and quizzes. Each one is drafted from your sources and comes with a citation you can click back to.

3

Review and approve

Skim the drafts in the review gate. Keep what's good, edit or reject the rest. Only approved items become part of your deck.

4

Remember it, calmly

Approved cards are scheduled with FSRS spaced repetition. Short, low-pressure sessions, and your tree keeps growing as you learn.

Requirements

Runs on your machine

No account, no cloud. On first launch, Arbora sets up its local AI engine and downloads a model sized for your computer.

Windows & macOS

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) or macOS on Apple Silicon. One installer, then just open it.

~8 GB RAM and up

Works on modest hardware with a smaller model; more memory unlocks larger, sharper models.

A few GB of disk

For the local AI engine and model. The one-time setup download runs entirely on your device.
